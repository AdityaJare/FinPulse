APPLE INC. - Q1 FY2025 FINANCIAL RESULTS & EARNINGS CALL HIGHLIGHTS
First Quarter Ended December 28, 2024
Source: SEC Form 10-Q / Apple Investor Relations

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & KEY FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS:
- Quarterly Revenue: $124.3 billion, an increase of 4% year-over-year compared to $119.6 billion in Q1 FY2024.
- All-time Record Diluted EPS: $2.40, up 10% year-over-year from $2.18.
- Gross Margin: $57.5 billion, resulting in a gross margin percentage of 46.2%, at the high end of company guidance.
- Net Income: $36.3 billion, up 7% compared to $33.9 billion in the year-ago quarter.
- Operating Cash Flow: Generated strong operating cash flow of $39.9 billion.
- Active Installed Base: Surpassed an all-time high of 2.35 billion active devices globally across all product categories.

REVENUE BY PRODUCT & SERVICES CATEGORIES:
1. iPhone:
   - Revenue was $69.1 billion, down 1% year-over-year compared to $69.7 billion.
   - Growth was strong in emerging markets including India, Latin America, and Southeast Asia.
   - Tim Cook (CEO) noted that iPhone 16 family powered by Apple Intelligence had faster upgrade momentum in markets where Apple Intelligence was deployed in English than previous iPhone cycles.

2. Services:
   - Revenue reached an all-time record of $26.3 billion, up 14% year-over-year from $23.1 billion.
   - Across App Store, Cloud Services, Apple Music, Apple Pay, and AppleCare, paid subscriptions surpassed 1.05 billion.
   - Services gross margin remained industry-leading at 74.2%.

3. Mac:
   - Revenue of $8.9 billion, an increase of 15% year-over-year from $7.8 billion, driven by strong customer reception of the redesigned M4 MacBook Pro, iMac, and Mac mini.

4. iPad:
   - Revenue was $8.1 billion, up 15% year-over-year, supported by the updated iPad Air and M4 iPad Pro line.

5. Wearables, Home and Accessories:
   - Revenue was $11.9 billion, down 1% year-over-year compared to $12.0 billion.

GEOGRAPHIC PERFORMANCE & GREATER CHINA:
- Americas revenue: $52.8 billion (up 5% YoY).
- Europe revenue: $32.7 billion (up 7% YoY).
- Greater China revenue: $18.5 billion, down 11% year-over-year from $20.8 billion.
  Luca Maestri (CFO) commentary on China:
  "While Greater China faced foreign exchange headwind of nearly 200 basis points and a highly competitive local smartphone market, our mainland China iPhone installed base reached an all-time high, with high retention rates and strong trade-in volumes."

CAPITAL RETURN PROGRAM:
- Returned nearly $30 billion to shareholders during the quarter:
  - $3.8 billion in cash dividends.
  - $24.8 billion through open-market repurchases of 110 million Apple shares.

FORWARD OUTLOOK FOR Q2 FY2025:
- Total company revenue expected to grow low to mid-single digits year-over-year.
- Services revenue expected to grow double digits at a rate similar to Q1.
- Gross margin expected to be between 46.0% and 47.0%.
- Operating expenses expected to be between $15.2 billion and $15.4 billion.
