MICROSOFT CORPORATION - Q2 FY2025 FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS & EARNINGS CALL
Fiscal Year 2025 Second Quarter Ended December 31, 2024
Source: SEC Form 10-Q / Microsoft Investor Relations

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CONSOLIDATED RESULTS:
- Total Quarterly Revenue: $69.6 billion, an increase of 12% in constant currency compared to the prior-year period.
- Operating Income: $31.6 billion, up 17% year-over-year. Operating margin expanded to 45.4%.
- Diluted Earnings Per Share (EPS): $3.23, an increase of 10% year-over-year.
- Microsoft Cloud Total Revenue: $38.9 billion, up 22% year-over-year, demonstrating sustained enterprise digital transformation momentum.
- Free Cash Flow: $9.1 billion, reflecting significant investments in technical infrastructure.

SEGMENT PERFORMANCE:
1. Intelligent Cloud:
   - Revenue was $29.9 billion, up 19% year-over-year.
   - Azure and other cloud services revenue grew 31% year-over-year (or 32% in constant currency).
   - Crucially, AI services contributed 12 points of Azure's 31% growth rate, up from 8 points in the same quarter last year.
   - Satya Nadella (Chairman and CEO) statement:
     "We have more than 60,000 Azure AI customers, up over 60% year-over-year, and average spend continues to increase. Microsoft 365 Copilot usage grew nearly 60% quarter-over-quarter, with over 70% of the Fortune 500 now using Copilot daily."

2. Productivity and Business Processes:
   - Revenue was $23.1 billion, up 13% year-over-year.
   - Office Commercial products and cloud services revenue increased 14%, driven by Office 365 Commercial revenue growth of 16%.
   - LinkedIn revenue increased 9%, propelled by AI-powered recruiting and talent tools.
   - Dynamics 365 revenue grew 18% on continued cloud ERP and CRM migrations.

3. More Personal Computing:
   - Revenue was $16.6 billion, up 3% year-over-year.
   - Windows OEM revenue was flat, while Xbox content and services revenue increased 13%, driven by Activision Blizzard integration and Xbox Game Pass record subscriber engagement.

CAPITAL EXPENDITURES & AI CAPACITY CONSTRAINTS:
- Amy Hood (Executive VP & CFO) Remarks:
  "Capital expenditures including assets acquired under finance leases were $19.0 billion in Q2, with cash paid for property, plant, and equipment of $14.1 billion. Roughly half of our cloud and AI CapEx was spent on long-lived data center land and building assets that will support monetization over the next 15 years, with the remainder dedicated to GPUs and networking hardware."
- Cloud Supply and Demand Dynamics:
  "Demand for Azure AI services continues to outpace available compute supply. We anticipate AI capacity constraints will persist through the third fiscal quarter, with capacity coming online progressively in Q4 to meet contracted customer backlog."

FORWARD GUIDANCE FOR Q3 FY2025:
- Intelligent Cloud revenue expected between $29.75 billion and $30.05 billion.
- Azure constant-currency growth guided at 31% to 32%.
- Capital expenditures projected to increase sequentially as AI buildout continues.
- Operating margin expected to be stable year-over-year.
